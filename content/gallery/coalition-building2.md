## YOU HAVE SHOWN US THE WAY

NOW MARCH WITH US ON DISABILITY INDEPENDENCE DAY
SUNDAY JULY 25, 1993
WHERE? Madison Ave., & 41st St

& First-ever Disability Pride Day
