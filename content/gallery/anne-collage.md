caption: "A color collage of six photographs arranged in two vertical columns documenting disability activist Anne Emerman's 1981 bus protest and subsequent use of wheelchair-accessible buses. In the left column, three photos show Emerman during her protest: at top, a New York City police officer leans over her as she reaches out to hold onto the front of a bus while another officer stands behind; in the middle, she sits in her wheelchair in front of MTA bus 2244 holding its windshield wiper to block it from moving; at bottom, she speaks into a yellow WBAI microphone surrounded by onlookers. In the right column, three photos show a smiling Emerman in a dark coat using a wheelchair lift at the doorway of a blue-and-white accessible bus featuring an advertisement reading \"MILK.
