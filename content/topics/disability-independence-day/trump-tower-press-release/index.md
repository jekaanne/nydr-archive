## The Disability Resistance

35 years ago, on July 26, 1990, the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) was signed into law by George H. W. Bush. The ADA was a milestone for the modern disability movement, which has been fighting for over half a century to improve the lives of people with all types of disability. Normally, we celebrate on this day. We are NOT celebrating today.

We are inside the Trump Tower because we want everyone to know that, like other marginalized groups in the United States today, we are under attack by Donald Trump and his fascist administration.

We are disability activists who refuse to allow our hard-won rights to be taken away. We refuse to allow our people to be hurt by cruel cuts to vital social programs like Medicaid for the benefit of billionaires. We refuse to allow the laws that we fiercely and tirelessly worked to get passed to be obliterated in the senseless dismantling of the American government and American democracy.

We are activists who have been made stronger by the experience of living with a disability in an ableist world. We are not afraid. We are ready to fight harder than we ever have fought before, and we invite all people of good conscience to join us. In the dying words of one of our leaders, Justin Dart, who was one of the architects of the ADA - We will lead on!
