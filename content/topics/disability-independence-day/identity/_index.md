In the 1990s, people argued over the best words to use. Some activists chose to take back old, hurtful words like cripple and call themselves "crips" to fight negative stereotypes. For example, British disability-rights singer Johnny Crescendo (pictured below) wore a famous t-shirt that read, "Piss on Pity." Nadina LaSpina later taught courses on disability identity and culture in one of the earliest disability studies courses at The New School.
