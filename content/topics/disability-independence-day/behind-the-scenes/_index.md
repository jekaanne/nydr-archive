Planning the march started in October 1992. The main organizers were members of the group Disabled In Action (DIA): Nadina LaSpina, Danny Robert, and Jerry Nuzzi. They did a lot of hard work behind the scenes. They created the event's goals, got government permits to close Madison Avenue, wrote to sponsors, and invited speakers. Having worked in the entertainment industry, Danny Robert recruited volunteers from television and theater to help build the stage and the parade float.
