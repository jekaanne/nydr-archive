Many Disabled In Action members helped organize the march, while the New York chapter of ADAPT was just getting started. Quadriplegic cartoonist John Callahan designed the official march t-shirt. His drawing features disabled people, including an amputee in a wheelchair and someone walking with crutches, holding signs. One sign read, “Proud to be blind, Deaf, disabled, cognitively challenged, epileptic…” and another read, “Vengan Todos” (Spanish for “Come All”).
