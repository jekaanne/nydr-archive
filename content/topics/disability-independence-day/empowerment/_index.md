Empowerment was a major theme for the march. The day was about celebrating independence and pride. It was also about the new legal power granted to disabled people and the strength that comes from being part of a proud community.

Top photo: March organizer, Jerry Nuzzi (front), during a demonstration earlier that summer. Kipp Watson, co-founder with Jerry of the 504 Democratic Club, is on the left and organized the first march in 1992. U.S. News & World Report, June 14, 1993
